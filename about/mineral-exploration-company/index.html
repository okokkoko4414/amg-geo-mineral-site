About AMG-GEO-Mineral

Our Story

Built for a New Resource Cycle

Mineral exploration is entering a new resource cycle. Discovery rates for Tier-1 deposits have been declining for two decades — and the margin for error in exploration targeting has never been thinner.

We founded AMG-GEO-Mineral to meet that challenge with a different model: full-cycle mineral exploration services — from generative reconnaissance through to resource definition — delivered by a senior team that averages 20+ years of field experience per geologist.

We are not a service company that takes instructions and delivers data. We are an exploration partner that brings geological judgment, operational discipline, and commercial awareness to every program.

Our model puts a single senior team in control of the full cycle. The same geologists who define the target criteria are in the field supervising the drilling and building the resource model. This continuity of geological thinking — from grassroots to feasibility — is the core of our approach. When stages of exploration are managed by disconnected teams, critical information is lost. We exist to close that gap.

Our capability spans the full spectrum of mineral exploration services:

  • Target generation and prospectivity analysis
  • Geophysical survey design and interpretation
  • Drilling program management (RC and diamond core)
  • Resource estimation and technical reporting (NI 43-101 / JORC)

We are currently evaluating partnership mandates for the upcoming field season and welcome discussions with exploration companies seeking senior technical capability without the overhead of an in-house team.

Our Methodology

How We Explore

Every exploration program is driven by a single question: what specific geological uncertainty are we trying to reduce? The answer defines every decision that follows.

🔍

Generate & Rank

Regional gap analysis and prospectivity mapping drive our target generation process. We integrate geological, geophysical, and geochemical datasets to produce ranked target pipelines, applying machine learning outputs as inputs to — not replacements for — geological reasoning. Every target carries a testable geological hypothesis before it enters the drill queue.

🏔

Survey & Model

Geophysical surveys are designed to the geological setting, not a template. From IP/resistivity arrays through airborne magnetics and EM, every survey is decision-ready — from instrument calibration through final inversion models. Data is integrated with structural geology and geochemistry to build three-dimensional geological models that directly inform drill targeting.

🧱

Drill & Refine

RC and diamond core drilling managed end-to-end with on-site geological supervision and real-time assay integration. Every meter drilled answers a specific geological question. Programs are adapted continuously as new data arrives, not reviewed only at milestone boundaries. This iterative approach reduces the meterage required to reach a resource definition decision.

⚳

Estimate & Report

Resource estimates built to JORC and NI 43-101 standards, structured to withstand competent-person review. Our geological modeling, geostatistics, and classification into Measured, Indicated, and Inferred categories follow the same principle as every other stage: the estimate must be defensible because the geological understanding that underpins it is sound.
