Our Services

Expertise Across the Mining Lifecycle

Comprehensive Geological & Mining Solutions

From grassroots exploration through resource estimation to environmental stewardship, we deliver integrated services that add value at every stage of your project.

⛨

Mineral Exploration

Our mineral exploration services span the full project lifecycle from regional reconnaissance to advanced drilling programs. We employ a systematic, data-driven approach to identify and evaluate mineral prospects with high economic potential. Our team integrates geophysical surveys, geochemical sampling, structural geology, and modern drilling techniques to deliver reliable exploration outcomes.

Target generation & ranking Geophysical & geochemical surveys Diamond & RC drilling programs Resource definition drilling
🗺

Geological Mapping

High-resolution geological mapping forms the foundation of every successful mining project. Our experienced geologists produce detailed maps at scales ranging from regional (1:100,000) to prospect (1:1,000), incorporating structural interpretation, lithological classification, and alteration mapping. We combine traditional field methods with remote sensing and drone-based surveys for comprehensive coverage.

Structural & lithological mapping Remote sensing & satellite imagery Drone-based photogrammetry 3D model generation
⛭

Geotechnical Analysis

Safe and efficient mining operations depend on robust geotechnical understanding. We provide comprehensive geotechnical assessments including rock mass characterization, slope stability analysis, ground support design, and foundation evaluation. Our analyses support both open-pit and underground mining operations, helping to mitigate risk and optimize extraction plans.

Rock mass classification Slope stability analysis Underground geotechnics Numerical modeling
🌱

Environmental Assessment

We are committed to responsible mining that respects environmental and social values. Our environmental assessment services include baseline studies, impact assessments, permitting support, and closure planning. We work closely with regulators, communities, and stakeholders to ensure projects meet the highest standards of environmental stewardship and social responsibility.

Environmental impact assessments Baseline water & biodiversity studies Permitting & regulatory compliance Mine closure & rehabilitation
📊

Data Analytics

Modern mining generates vast quantities of data. Our analytics team transforms this data into actionable intelligence through advanced statistical analysis, machine learning, and geospatial modeling. We build custom dashboards, predictive models, and decision-support tools that improve exploration targeting, optimize grade control, and enhance operational efficiency across the mining value chain.

Machine learning for targeting Geostatistical modeling GIS & remote sensing analysis Custom data visualization
⚳

Resource Estimation

Accurate resource estimation is critical for project valuation, mine planning, and investor confidence. Our team delivers JORC, NI 43-101, and CRIRSCO-compliant resource estimates using industry-leading software and robust geological modeling techniques. We provide all aspects of estimation from data compilation and validation to block modeling and classification.

JORC & NI 43-101 compliant estimates Block modeling & grade interpolation Resource classification Audit & due diligence support
