Mineral Exploration Services

Full-Cycle Capability

Four Disciplines, One Integrated Team

Full-cycle mineral exploration capability is not about offering a menu of services. It is about integrating every stage so that decisions made during targeting directly inform drill programs. At AMG-GEO-Mineral, we structure our exploration services around four core disciplines — each designed to reduce technical risk and accelerate discovery timelines.

Service 01

Exploration Targeting

Systematic target generation that begins with regional geology and ends with ranked, drill-ready prospects.

Our exploration targeting process combines traditional field geology with modern data science to identify and rank mineral prospects with high economic potential. We begin with regional gap analysis and prospectivity mapping, integrating geological, geophysical, and geochemical datasets to define the mineral system context. Structural interpretation at multiple scales — from regional lineament analysis to prospect-scale structural mapping — constrains the geological framework within which mineralisation may occur.

Machine learning and data-driven methods are applied as inputs to geological reasoning, not as replacements for it. We do not treat algorithm outputs as answers; they are pattern-recognition tools that highlight areas deserving closer geological scrutiny. Every target in our pipeline carries a testable geological hypothesis before it advances to the next stage.

Our targeting workflow delivers a ranked target pipeline with clear economic and geological rationale for each prospect, enabling our clients to allocate exploration budgets with confidence.

Service 02

Geophysical Surveys

Survey design grounded in deposit-model-specific rationale — every method chosen for the physical property contrast it measures, at the depth and resolution required.

⚡

Induced Polarization & Resistivity

The most widely applied ground geophysical method for disseminated sulphide exploration. Measures chargeability and resistivity to detect sulphide minerals directly. We configure IP programs across dipole-dipole (150–300 m depth), pole-dipole (300–500 m), and 3D arrays depending on target requirements. Data is processed through iterative inversion modelling to produce true-resistivity and chargeability-depth sections integrated with geological models for ranked drill targeting.

🌍

Airborne & Ground Magnetics

The fastest, most cost-effective method for regional reconnaissance. Modern high-resolution surveys resolve individual dykes, fault offsets below 10 m, and subtle magnetic susceptibility contrasts between lithological units. Processing includes reduced-to-pole transforms, upward/downward continuation, derivative products for structural lineament mapping, and 3D magnetic inversion for depth-to-source estimation. Applied across the full exploration lifecycle from structural framework definition to direct targeting of alteration halos.

📡

Electromagnetic Surveys

Primary tool for direct detection of massive sulphide deposits. Time-domain EM systems (VTEM, SkyTEM, ground TEM) deliver deep penetration of 300–500 m with conductive-body discrimination through decay-profile analysis. Frequency-domain EM provides superior shallow resolution for near-surface conductors. Our interpretation goes beyond conductor detection to conductor classification — distinguishing massive sulphide lenses from graphitic shales or saline groundwater through careful analysis of response geometry and decay characteristics.

⛰

Gravity & Seismic Methods

For deep-crustal architecture and covered-basin exploration, gravity surveys measure density contrasts from mafic intrusions, massive sulphide bodies, and ultramafic sequences. Seismic reflection — adapted from petroleum exploration — images deep structures and orebodies at kilometre-scale depths. While more expensive than potential-field methods, seismic delivers the depth resolution essential for brownfield exploration beneath existing workings or in covered terrain.

At AMG-GEO-Mineral, every geophysics program begins with a question: what specific geological uncertainty are we trying to reduce? The answer defines the method selection, survey parameters, and interpretation pathway. The output is not a map or an inversion model — it is a drill target with a defined geological rationale and a testable hypothesis.

Our team of senior geophysicists brings combined experience across Archean greenstone belts, Proterozoic sedimentary basins, Cordilleran orogenic belts, Andean porphyry systems, and Phanerozoic volcanic arcs. Every survey is designed to the geological setting, not a template.

Service 03

Drilling Programs

End-to-end drilling program management — from hole planning through on-site supervision to real-time assay integration and adaptive program refinement.

Our drilling programs are structured so that every meter drilled answers a specific geological question. We manage both RC and diamond core drilling programs end-to-end, providing complete hole planning that integrates geological models, geophysical targets, and geochemical anomalism into optimal drill collar locations and orientations.

On-site geological supervision is provided by senior geologists who understand the deposit model and can make real decisions at the rig. Logging protocols capture lithological, structural, and alteration data at the level of detail required for resource estimation. Assay results are integrated continuously, enabling adaptive program refinement — the drilling program evolves as new data arrives, rather than being reviewed only at milestone boundaries.

This iterative approach reduces the meterage required to reach a resource definition decision. Our team has directly supervised over 180,000 m of diamond and RC drilling across base metals, precious metals, and critical minerals in West Africa, Southeast Asia, the Canadian Shield, and the Andean region.

Service 04

Resource Estimation

JORC and NI 43-101 compliant resource estimates built to withstand competent-person review.

Accurate resource estimation is critical for project valuation, mine planning, and investor confidence. Our team delivers JORC, NI 43-101, and CRIRSCO-compliant resource estimates using industry-leading software and robust geological modeling techniques. We provide all aspects of estimation from data compilation and validation to block modeling, grade interpolation, and resource classification.

Our resource estimation workflow is built on the same principle as every other stage of our service model: the estimate must be defensible because the geological understanding that underpins it is sound. Geological modeling defines the mineralised domains; geostatistical analysis constrains the interpolation parameters; and classification into Measured, Indicated, and Inferred categories follows established guidelines with full transparency on the assumptions and risk factors involved.

We also provide independent audit and due diligence support for companies requiring third-party review of existing resource estimates, technical reports, or competent-person opinions.
